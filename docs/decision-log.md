# Phygitals Aggregator - Decision Log

Every question the requirements depend on, and every decision taken. A row is **Open** until someone with the authority answers it; it then becomes **Confirmed**, with the date and who decided. A question that turns out to be engineering's to answer is marked **Tech** and moves to that module's SDD.

IDs are stable: stories cite them (`TBC — see Q-11`). Never renumber.

Read with G4, exclusivity covers every place Patrianna shows a pack or a card: pack lists, pack contents and chase cards, previews, recent wins and any feed. What a brand-only pack may contain, and whether it must stay off Phygitals' own storefront, is split out as Q-33. |

| Q-13 | **Exclusive cards later on.** When a player sells back a brand's exclusive card, when a brand leaves, or when its pack is retired, where do those cards go? | Confirmed 2026-10-28 (product) | **Patrianna owns the exclusive cards it buys for a brand.** When a brand leaves or retires a brand-only pack, Patrianna decides what happens to the cards nobody pulled: move them into another brand's pack, or sell them back to Phygitals. ## Open — shipping and compliance

| ID | Decision required | Status | Outcome |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Q-14 | **Who is the sponsor.** Who is "the Company" in the legal opinion — Patrianna, or each brand's operating company? Does each partner brand need its own certification? | Confirmed 2026-10-28 (product) | **Each brand's operating company is the sponsor, with its own certification.** This holds for own brands and partner brands alike. Consequences: a brand cannot go live before its company's certification is in place; each brand's player-facing terms name its own sponsor; compliance records (shipping charges, delivery times, FMV at the rip) must be producible per brand, because each sponsor answers for its own players. |
| Q-15 | **Shipping fee.** Does the player pay the actual carrier cost plus a handling fee no higher than the actual handling cost and USD 5? Is Phygitals' withdrawal fee, shown today as the "handling fee", a handling fee under that cap? Is there any free-shipping rule? | Confirmed 2026-10-28 (product) | **Carrier cost plus handling, with caps and free-delivery rules applied state by state.** Everywhere, the charge is based on what shipping actually costs (compliance input 1). Where a state limits charges, its rule applies to that player's shipment: in Virginia the handling fee may not exceed the actual handling cost and is at most USD 5. No general free-shipping rule; free delivery only where a state requires it (Q-18). This makes the per-state rule set (Q-16) a launch dependency. Whether Phygitals' withdrawal fee counts as handling is split out as Q-29. |
| Q-16 | **State rules.** Is Virginia the only state known so far? Who maintains the per-state rules (fee caps, free delivery, deadlines, exclusions), and how often do they change? | Confirmed 2026-10-28 (product) | **Legal decides the rules; Patrianna operations keeps them current in the back office.** A rule change takes effect without a release. Virginia is the only state known so far; the full list is Q-30. |
| Q-30 | **Which states have shipping rules, and what are they.** Split from Q-16. Legal to list every state that limits shipping charges or sets a delivery deadline, with the rule for each, before launch. Virginia: delivery within 10 days of the win notification, no expense to the winner, handling fee at most the actual cost and USD 5. Since 2026-10-28 also the excluded states, for buying and for shipping (Q-02). | Open | |
| Q-17 | **Delivery deadline.** Virginia requires delivery within 10 days of the win notification. What counts as the notification in our flow — the reveal or the shipping request? What deadline applies elsewhere? Who is accountable when Phygitals' vault misses it? | Confirmed 2026-10-28 (product) | **Delivery within 10 days of the shipping request, in every state.** A card is claimed when the player requests its shipment, and the clock starts then. The strictest known deadline applies everywhere, so there is no deadline per state; Q-30 still has to flag any state stricter than 10 days. Every shipping order must therefore be trackable against its deadline, and operations must learn of an order at risk before the deadline passes. Whether Phygitals commits to it is split out as Q-32. |
| Q-32 | **Phygitals' delivery commitment.** Split from Q-17. Does Phygitals commit, in the contract, to delivering within 10 days of the shipping request (vault preparation plus carrier)? What happens when it misses: remedy, credit, who tells the player? Commercial / Phygitals to answer. | Open | |
| Q-18 | **"No obligation or expense to the winner" (Virginia).** Packs are bought with US dollars (Q-05). Is a card pulled from a paid pack a "prize" under the Virginia rule? If so, must shipping be free for Virginia players, for every card? And may a card the player has not claimed be sold back automatically when its window ends (Q-26)? | Open | |
| Q-19 | **FMV at the rip.** When a pack is bought now and opened later, is the rip the purchase or the opening? What is FMV used for: display only, prize-value disclosure, sell-back value, tax reporting? | Confirmed 2026-10-28 (product) | **The rip is the purchase.** Cards are drawn when the pack is bought, so each card's FMV is fixed at that moment and never changes afterwards, even when the player opens the pack later. Baseline: Partial — Collectibles reads FMV after the purchase and retries for up to a day ([Current State](current-state.md)); the value kept must be the one at the purchase. What FMV is used for is split out as Q-31. |
| Q-31 | **What FMV is used for.** Split from Q-19. Display to the player only? Prize-value disclosure? A basis for the sell-back price or for shipping insurance? Tax reporting thresholds? Legal to answer. | Open | |
| Q-20 | **Player checks.** KYC, age and location before buying or shipping: does the brand platform own them, or must the aggregator enforce them? Is shipping outside the US allowed? | Confirmed 2026-10-28 (product) | **The brand platform runs KYC, age and location checks, as today; the aggregator checks each shipping address.** A player the brand lets in may buy. At shipping, the aggregator checks the destination against the state rules (Q-16, Q-30) and the excluded states (Q-02) before the order is placed. Shipping outside the US moved to Q-02. |
