**Subject:** Phygitals packs on our brands — rulings needed before launch

Hi,

We are adding Phygitals card packs to several of our brands, all through one Phygitals account. Players buy packs with their US-dollar balance. The cards stay in Phygitals' insured vault until the player either sells them back or asks for them to be shipped. Players and shipping are US only.

Your review of the officer's certificate is built into the requirements. To recap it: shipping fees must be reasonable and based on actual cost; claimed cards must ship within the required timeframes; Virginia requires delivery within 10 days of notification at no expense to the winner, with a handling fee no higher than actual cost and capped at USD 5; and FMV is fixed at the Rip. To finish the requirements we need the following.

**A. Please confirm how we have read your review**

1. **Sponsor.** Each brand's own operating company is the sponsor ("the Company") for that brand's players, and each needs its own officer's certificate.
2. **Claimed, and the deadline.** A card is claimed when the player asks for it to be shipped. We deliver within 10 days of that request in every state, applying Virginia's deadline everywhere.
3. **The Rip.** The cards in a pack are drawn when the pack is bought, even if the player opens it later. We treat the purchase as the Rip and fix each card's FMV at that moment.
4. **Shipping charge.** The player pays the actual carrier cost plus a handling fee. Where a state limits charges, its rule applies; in Virginia the handling fee is at most the actual handling cost and at most USD 5. There is no general free-shipping offer.

**B. Questions**

5. Is a card pulled from a pack the player **paid for** a "prize" under Virginia's rule? If so, must shipping be free for Virginia players, for every card?
6. **Automatic sell-back.** If a player neither sells nor ships a card, it is sold back automatically when the brand's window ends — 72 hours by default, with a warning 24 hours before — and the player is credited Phygitals' buyback price. Is this allowed for each sponsor? In Virginia, given "no obligation to the winner"?
7. Phygitals' shipping quote includes a **"withdrawal fee"**. Is it a handling fee under the state caps, part of the carrier cost, or neither? We are asking Phygitals what it covers.
8. Please list the states that:
   a. limit shipping or handling charges, and how;
   b. set a delivery deadline, especially any shorter than 10 days;
   c. require delivery at no cost to the winner;
   d. must be excluded from buying, from shipping, or both.
9. Beyond showing it to the player, what is **FMV** used for? For example prize-value disclosure, the basis for sell-back or shipping insurance, or tax reporting thresholds such as 1099s — and is that per sponsor?
10. How long must we keep **compliance records**: each shipment's charge, actual cost, rule applied, request date and delivery date, and each card's FMV at purchase?

Thanks.
